Questions, answered.

Does Attest replace my auditor?
No. Only licensed CPA firms can issue SOC 2 reports, and auditors must be independent of the tool vendor. Attest gets you audit-ready—monitored controls, collected evidence, an auditor-ready package—and you bring your licensed CPA firm. We're building a partner network so we can introduce you when you're ready.
How long does it take to get audit-ready?
Most startups get audit-ready in weeks, not months. That's the honest limit of what any tool can claim: SOC 2 Type II still requires a 6–12 month observation window set by your auditor. What Attest shortens is the preparation—and it keeps you in a passing state for the whole window instead of scrambling before the audit.
What integrations does Attest connect to?
At launch: AWS, GitHub, and Google Workspace, connected via read-only OAuth scopes—Attest can observe your environment, never change it. New integrations ship based on what customers actually ask for.
What does the AI-agent audit trail actually record?
For each agent action: what ran, when, what it touched, which control it maps to, and who approved it (a human approver or a reviewed policy). Actions that break policy are flagged or blocked in real time—so the export you hand your auditor is complete, not reconstructed from memory.
Is my data safe?
We take a minimal approach: read-only integrations, encryption in transit and at rest, and only the evidence your audit requires. You can export or delete your data anytime—disconnect an integration and the collection stops immediately.
Can I cancel monthly?
Yes. Starter and Growth are month-to-month with no annual lock-in. Cancel in the app whenever you want; your data stays available for export through the end of your billing period.
